Client file · what targeting info is public?

The library shows the ads. It hides the audience.

What anyone can see (we verified)

  • Creative, copy, CTA, landing domain, start/stop dates, platforms, active status.
  • Collation counts — how many near-duplicate ads sit behind one creative. Liquid Death's biggest: 113 variants behind a single collated ad. That's a targeting/batch footprint, visible if you know to look.
  • Catalog plumbing: {{product.brand}} templates, Amazon retail links, creator-style copy ("For the mommas…").

What the US library will not tell you

  • No age, gender, location, or interest targeting for commercial ads — our pull returned empty country targeting and only boilerplate regulation flags.
  • No spend, impressions, or reach for commercial ads (political ads are a different, transparent world — remember the Russian-ads data from the Meta lecture's neighborhood).
  • So: competitor creative is free; competitor strategy must be inferred.
